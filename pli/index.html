Principal Investigator · Laboratory for Sleep and Digital Health

Dr. Peng Li

  • Assistant Professor Massachusetts General Hospital, Harvard Medical School
  • Associate Member Broad Institute of MIT & Harvard

Dr. Peng Li directs the Laboratory for Sleep and Digital Health and is Research Director of the Medical Biodynamics Center at Massachusetts General Hospital. His research bridges sleep and circadian physiology with biomedical engineering, using wearable and physiological signals to understand cognitive aging and resilience.
